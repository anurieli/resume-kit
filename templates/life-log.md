# Life log

Answers one question, over and over, for as long as this workspace exists:

> **What are you up to right now, or what have you been up to?**

Newest entry first. One entry per check-in. Keep it in the person's own
words, loose and unstructured. This is not a status report and it does not
need to be tidy.

## Why this exists

A career record goes stale quietly. Someone changes roles, ships something
big, gets handed a team, starts consulting on the side, and none of it lands
in `career.yaml` because nobody sat down to do an interview. Then a job comes
up, they generate a resume, and it describes who they were a year ago.

This log is the cheap fix. It costs a minute and it gives the next
enrichment session something real to pull on.

## The rule every skill follows

Any resume-kit skill, when it starts, reads the top entry's date.

- **Less than 2 months old:** carry on, say nothing.
- **2 months old or more, or the log is empty:** ask the question above
  before doing anything else. Write the answer here as a new entry. Then
  mine it: concrete events become `self-reported` material in `career.yaml`,
  opinions about themselves become `self-assessment`, and anything that
  deserves a longer telling gets its own file in `reflections/`.

One question, not an interrogation. If the answer opens something up, that
is what `career-enrich` is for; suggest it rather than turning the check-in
into a full session.
