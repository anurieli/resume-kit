Senior Backend Engineer — Platform Reliability
Company: Delta Freight Systems

We're looking for a senior backend engineer to own the reliability of our
event-driven order processing pipeline. You'll work primarily in Python,
with heavy use of Kafka for streaming, and Postgres for durable state.

What you'll do:
- Own and improve the reliability of a high-throughput event-driven
  pipeline (Kafka-based).
- Reduce error rates and processing latency in mission-critical
  reconciliation workflows.
- Mentor engineers on the team and help establish on-call/reliability
  practices.

What we're looking for:
- 5+ years backend engineering experience, Python preferred.
- Direct experience with Kafka or similar event-streaming systems.
- Track record of measurably improving reliability or performance
  (numbers, not adjectives).
- Comfortable mentoring other engineers.
